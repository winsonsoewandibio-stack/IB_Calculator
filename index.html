Diploma award requirements

The estimate checks these conditions:

  • At least 24 total points, including core bonus points.
  • A grade in all six subjects, the Extended Essay and Theory of Knowledge; CAS requirements completed.
  • No subject grade of 1; no more than two grade 2s overall; and no more than three grades of 3 or below overall.
  • At least 12 points across HL subjects (if taking four HL subjects, the three highest HL grades are counted).
  • At least 9 points across three SL subjects, or at least 5 points across two SL subjects when taking four HL subjects.
  • Neither the Extended Essay nor Theory of Knowledge is grade E.
  • Six distinct courses: an interdisciplinary course cannot be entered or counted twice.

This is a planning check, not an official diploma award. Additional IB rules, including academic integrity and course-combination requirements, may apply. Confirm with your DP coordinator and the official DP passing criteria.
